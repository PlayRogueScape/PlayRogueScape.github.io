The Codex — the systems behind RogueScape

Every system in this game is a wave-survival roguelike mechanic wearing Gielinor's skin. Gear stats use the design wiki's affix system — attack/spell damage %, crit, armor, evasion, luck, pickup range, summon damage — and the rarest rolls in that design are carried by the rarest, most iconic items in RogueScape.

Loot rarities

Simple (grey) → Common (white) → Rare (blue) → Epic (purple) → Mythic (red), plus gold UNIQUE items with game-changing effects, dropped by elites and bosses. Higher rarity = more affixes, stronger rolls. The material ladder follows RogueScape: Simple = Bronze, Common = Iron/Steel, Rare = Mithril/Adamant/Rune, Epic = Dragon & Barrows, Mythic = endgame rares.

Enemies — weakest to strongest

The horde's fodder is played by Gielinor's weakest monsters: Chickens, Giant Rats, Goblins and Cows first, then Spiders, Skeletons, Zombies and fast Wolves. Mid-game brings Hill Giants, Moss Giants and Black Knights. Elites are Lesser and Greater Demons; the mini-boss is a Greater Demon Champion; and the final boss is the King Black Dragon.
